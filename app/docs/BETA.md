# Running a beta with real customers

How to give Lead Autopilot to a few customers, get useful feedback in 2–3 weeks, and know what to fix.

## Set up each customer (15 minutes)

1. **Deploy one copy per customer** (DEPLOY.md, Option A or B). Each gets its own address, password and data: nobody sees anyone else's leads.
2. Set `FEEDBACK_EMAIL` to your address: a **Feedback** button appears in their header and emails you what they were doing.
3. Add their keys if they have them (Google, Serper, Gemini); otherwise yours, with a Google budget alert.
4. Under **Clients**, add their business from their website (the app drafts the profile). Check services, prices and the message rules with them.
5. Send them: the address, the password (separately, e.g. by phone), and the 3 things to try below.

## The first call (20 minutes, share screen)

1. **One search together**: their city and area, the niche that fits them (website, logistics, marketing, solar, CA/GST, staffing, insurance), 2–3 business types.
2. While it runs, open a strong lead: read the "why now" line and the reasons together. Ask: *would you call this business?*
3. Edit the first message into their voice, send one from their own WhatsApp, mark it contacted.
4. Show **Report** (a PDF they can forward) and **Export** (CSV).

## Ask them to try

- Run 3 searches in different areas or business types.
- Message the top 10 leads over a week; mark replies and wrong details (a note on the lead: "wrong number", "closed", "not the owner").
- Use the Feedback button whenever something is confusing, wrong or missing.

## What to ask at the end of week 1 and week 2

1. Of the leads marked strong, how many would you really contact? Which ones were wrong, and why?
2. How many phone numbers or emails were wrong or reached the wrong person?
3. Were the "why now" reasons true when you checked? Which one was most convincing to the business owner?
4. How many messages did you send, and how many replied? Which message worked best?
5. What did you have to do outside the app (copying, looking things up, a spreadsheet)?
6. What would make you pay for this, and how much a month?
7. Which other kinds of business or services should it find leads for?

## What to watch (in each customer's app)

| Signal | Where | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Searches run, leads contacted | Home | they come back on their own after week 1 |
| Reply rate, strong vs worth a try | Home → Reply rate | strong at least twice worth-a-try |
| Wrong contact details | notes on leads, CSV "Contact confidence" | under 1 in 10 contacted |
| Email bounces | Outreach → Email | under 2% |

After 30 messaged leads in one niche, scores start learning from their replies (the "Your results" line on a lead), so the second week's lists should already fit them better.

## Known limits to tell them up front

- The free map (OpenStreetMap) has far fewer businesses than Google Maps in India; a Google key or Serper finds many more.
- Facebook Page search needs Meta's approval; until then Facebook pages are found through web search only.
- Emails go out slowly on purpose (warm-up, office hours) to keep the sending domain out of spam.
- First WhatsApp messages go from their own phone: WhatsApp bans numbers that message strangers through the API.
